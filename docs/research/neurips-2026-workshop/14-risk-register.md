# 14 — Protocol v2 Risk Register

**Status:** live execution control, revised 2026-07-22. Canonical science is
`02-research-thesis.md`; `16-protocol-v2-hardening.md` records the red-team.
Primary venue: Verify-Agents at NeurIPS 2026 in Sydney, Australia, deadline
2026-08-29 AoE. IAB is fallback only.

Scientific findings are not software acceptance tests. A negative effect is a
valid result; broken randomization, label leakage, firewall failure, corrupted
artifacts, or invalid inference is not.

Severity: `critical` can invalidate the headline; `high` can force a short/
methods paper; `medium` weakens scope but is recoverable. Likelihood is reassessed
after each stage gate.

## 1. Validity and measurement risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Early trigger | Prevention / validity gate | Honest contingency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Exposure is foreign/scripted, treatment-dependent, or coupled to an arm-specific denominator/filesystem history. | medium | critical | Exposure comes from another model; eligible tasks differ by arm; a never-failing arm is dropped; opportunity snapshots inherit different files. | Require the same frozen subject's live verified pretreatment failure under one common untreated prefix; clone before arm mapping; start every scheduled opportunity from its own digest-bound snapshot with only declared memory/state persistent; immutable common denominator; every branch-local failure adverse. | Invalidate affected runs and rerun. Scripted/foreign exposures remain engineering fixtures; never rescue by conditioning on observed failure. |
| M-02 | The recognizer smuggles prior-task memory or the same label path administers treatment and declares notice/outcome. | high | critical | Recognizer sees receipt/history; notice label changes after action; imports/parameters/thresholds are shared with updater/evaluator; evaluator sees state or arm. | Recognizer is current-task/within-opportunity only; generator-held recurrence target freezes before action; `NoticeProbability`, `LearningSignal`, actuation `L_m`, and arm-blind outcome evaluator use separate schemas/packages/tuning/parameters; pseudonymous arms and mutation tests. | Restrict the endpoint to exact independent labels or ship a measurement-validity paper; make no “noticed recurrence” claim. |
| M-03 | Counterfactual branch freezes treatment descendants and becomes off-policy. | medium | critical | Later prompt, tool output, file state, or retrieved memory is replayed after actions diverge. | Exact equality only through `t0`; clone initial sandbox/prefix/config/RNG streams; every branch executes live; assert divergence begins no earlier than `t0`. | Drop invalid intervention runs and rerun; deterministic replay remains engineering evidence only. |
| M-04 | Pneuma is merely a simple persistent scalar controller. | high | critical | A last-bit/count/EWMA/Beta controller matches Pneuma; state variables add no action/outcome effect. | Mandatory scalar arm selected from the published four-family grid with the same current-only recognizer, signal, scope, precision, receipt rules, head/caps/task order, and at least Pneuma's development-trial budget; freeze the complete search ledger before pilot. | Report the clean negative: simple persistent state helps, but richer state provides no demonstrated benefit. |
| M-05 | Prototype pseudoreplication, invalid assignment replay, or duplicate multiplicity inflates confidence. | medium | critical | Analysis treats variants/challenges/task×seed as independent, sign-flips contrasts, confuses Fisher sharp-null evidence with average-effect intervals, selects the best observed baseline, or tests H5 twice. | Highest shared authored prototype/generator lineage is independent; lineage digests enter split/power/analysis; `M ~ Uniform(7!)` maps policies to fixed streams independently of stream order `O ~ Uniform(7!)`; ≥100k Fisher draws condition on recorded `O`, replay `M`, and re-aggregate after every draw; ≥10k motif-stratified authored-lineage bootstrap draws provide studentized max-T population-average bounds; H1–H4 form explicit IUTs, all four Holm slots remain present (`p=1` if invalid/unavailable/inconclusive), and H5 is embedded only in H1. | Recompute from the recorded assignment and equal cell→lineage→motif hierarchy; if assignment was not randomized, suppress Fisher/randomized-causal claims and use only the preregistered lineage-cluster fallback; downgrade unsupported claims. |
| M-06 | E-0 length/activity confound reappears through hidden context, compute, or fallback budget. | high | critical | Arms differ in candidate/reflection/maintenance/repair calls, failed-call treatment, truncation, tools, or retries; repeat harm tracks trajectory length. | One common candidate call and deterministic truncation; complete common caps/accounting for candidate, reflection, memory/state maintenance, repair/fallback, input/output tokens, attempted tools, retries, and failed calls; Retry-count arm; shuffled-text/state-serialization diagnostics; resource–utility frontiers; no primary adjustment for realized resources. | Characterize mediation/confound and report negative/inconclusive causal evidence. |
| M-07 | Agent games recurrence by refusing, timing out, stopping early, crashing one branch, or substituting a new failure. | high | critical | Lower target recurrence with worse engagement, success, or total failure. | Every observed agent-side non-engagement or branch-local runtime failure is adverse; only a strictly evidenced exogenous whole-block outage is excludable; mandatory 0.05 non-inferiority gates for engagement/success/total failure/false avoidance; decoys and counterfactuals. | H1 fails even if raw recurrence falls; describe avoidance/failure substitution. |
| M-08 | Retrieval/reflection baselines are weak or unequally implemented. | medium | high | Baseline success far below dev anchors; extra repair calls/caps differ; memory records malformed. | Genuine deterministic top-k semistructured retrieval and fixed-budget reflect/store/recondition loop; shared evidence, head/candidates, tuning budget, and caps; dev diagnostics. | Disclose baseline deficit; do not claim superiority over the affected class. |
| M-09 | Standardized exposure or B-live evidence is generalized beyond its target. | high | high | Effect disappears in natural sequences; receipt is unrealistically informative; a small engineered SWE-Gym subset is called natural SWE. | Receipt contains only legitimate observables from the same subject's own failed run; report secondary natural-sequence policy value and B-offline prevalence separately; define B-live's target as the frozen mechanically scorable known-motif repository/prototype population. | Scope the causal conclusion to behaviour after a standardized verified failure and B-live transfer only to its preregistered population. |
| M-10 | H3 reporter decodes intervention metadata/receipts or sparse classes rather than explaining a verified behavioural change. | medium | high | Reporter identity/checkpoint drifts; it sees target-specific fields; the chance baseline assumes fewer than nine labels; high accuracy occurs without action/outcome divergence; rare directions have no support; receipt-only decoder ties. | Invoke the same frozen actor checkpoint only in a separate post-behaviour, tool-free, separately metered call; use nine labels (four variables×two directions + `no_attributable_change`), a target-symmetric public schema with operation/target/assignment fields withheld, receipt-only decoder plus uniform/prior baselines, frozen macro accuracy/recall, minimum coverage, risk–coverage, conditional direction/no-change diagnostics, report-disabled null, and the prose firewall. | H3 is inconclusive if identity/timing, support/coverage, or firewall gates fail or decoder performance explains it; report grounding only and make no causal-attribution claim. |
| M-11 | Generalization language outruns the mechanism. | medium | high | Per-motif table is claimed to handle never-seen motifs; holdout components leak. | Confirmatory H4 is known motif across new surfaces/repos; explicit separated split regimes and quarantine; unseen motif is exploratory unless a new generalizer is preregistered. | State the tested boundary and negative unseen-motif result. |
| M-12 | Pilot/tuning leakage contaminates confirmatory data. | medium | critical | Pilot outcomes alter motifs, transforms, checkpoint rules, notice/evaluator criteria, margins, hyperparameters, or controller grids; pilot lineages reappear in confirmation. | Freeze every criterion, margin, transform, and controller grid on development data before pilot; pilot uses disjoint authored lineages and exposes pooled/blinded nuisance variance, event rates, ICC, detector error, and attrition only under a deterministic sample-size/infeasibility rule; hash manifests and exclude all pilot lineages/surfaces. | Replace contaminated units before unblinding or abandon the confirmatory label; never retroactively call an outcome-informed pilot confirmatory. |
| M-13 | Influence-off is mistaken for evidence that cross-task persistence caused the effect. | medium | critical | Influence-off changes behaviour, but resetting state after exposure has no effect, is omitted, or state is reconstructed from the retained receipt. | Mandatory persistence-off reset immediately after exposure and before the opportunity, preserving current task, candidates, and head while actor/updater/carrier/head receive only a same-shape inert handle through the first decision; the immutable receipt remains on the audit/report path only and cannot rehydrate state; branch failure to reach opportunity remains adverse ITT. | Credit state actuation at most; do not claim persistent carryover if the reset does not remove the preregistered advantage. |
| M-14 | A non-significant stochastic clone/null difference is misreported as equivalence. | medium | critical | Too few draws; RNG/support differs across arms; sham/no-op/restore null merely has `p>0.05`. | Freeze categorical support and equivalence margins; use symmetric RNG, powered repeated draws, TV/JS summaries, and sham/no-op/restore distributions; bind model/checkpoint/prompt/tool environment and report power. | Treat the stochastic causal gate as failed/inconclusive; byte equality remains an engineering fixture result only. |

## 2. Engineering and data risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Early trigger | Prevention / validity gate | Honest contingency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T-01 | Live structured-action driver does not exist or cannot enforce equality. | high | critical | Conditions require different prompts/calls/repair paths; trace lacks candidate scores. | Build one seed-pinned driver first; full candidate/action receipts; condition module limited to defined interface; differential tests across zero-pressure arms. | Use only deterministic instrumentation and submit no live-agent claim until fixed. |
| T-02 | Exact motif rules lack precision/coverage. | high | critical | Metamorphic transforms change labels; blinded human review disagrees; opportunities not observable pre-action. | Start with 4–6 mechanically auditable motifs; fixtures and mutation/property tests; recognizer/evaluator measured separately; semantic motifs secondary. | Narrow the taxonomy and claims; report coverage rather than inventing labels. |
| T-03 | Decision head violates “pressure, not commands” or gives arms unequal action sets. | medium | critical | Head edits tool/arguments, adds candidates, or condition-specific fallback occurs. | One common model call; fixed `K`; immutable candidate tool/args; bounded meta-class bias only; zero-vector equivalence and invalid-JSON/fallback parity tests. | Invalidate runs; reduce head to a verifiable reranker. |
| T-04 | Runnable repository sandboxes are unavailable or non-reproducible. | high | high | No image/snapshot; tests fail before agent acts; network-dependent setup; repo licensing unclear. | Suite A local micro-repos first; acquire a focused SWE-Gym subset into isolated digest-bound images; preflight base commits/tests/licenses. | Keep B-live as qualified external validation or omit it; never call Open-SWE a live suite. |
| T-05 | State update semantics are circular, unstable, or collapse to zero/saturation. | medium | high | `r_m` uses evaluator labels; `L` nonzero at neutral; values saturate; decay scales with actions. | Actor-side diagnostics only; dev-fitted frozen reliability/Beta update; task-boundary decay; caps/centering; state-machine properties and dose tests; four-variable/nine-label cardinality is fail-closed. | Stop during development. Removing `r_m` requires a new decision-log amendment before pilot and a synchronized three-variable/seven-label protocol and power update; there is no automatic fallback. |
| T-06 | Seed control is weaker than claimed on GPU/model server. | medium | high | Same seed yields different prefixes; server batching changes sampling; backend version drift. | Pin checkpoint/quantization/server/sampler/batching; seed audit; deterministic fixtures separate; distributional inference for live model; log backend/hardware. | Describe stochasticity honestly and increase cluster/seed uncertainty; never claim byte determinism. |
| T-07 | Raw trajectory re-extraction leaks secrets/PII or violates source governance. | medium | critical | Raw issue/tool text reaches public frames; secret scanner hits; license provenance missing. | Read raw data only in governed adapters; transient text; typed/digested public frames; secret/PII scan; data card and license ledger; no writes to `C:\pneuma-data`. | Withhold affected artifacts, publish transforms/manifests, and restrict analysis to safe fields. |
| T-08 | Trace/provenance artifacts are incomplete or internally inconsistent. | medium | critical | Missing condition/config/task/model/seed hashes; orphan receipts; unblinding map leaks. | Versioned JSON Schemas, referential-integrity validator, atomic writers, content hashes, pseudonymous ids, official-run fail-closed gate. | Mark run invalid before analysis; no manual repair after unblinding. |
| T-09 | Current test baseline remains red or platform-specific. | high | high | Fresh WSL suite reproduces known failures; CRLF/path/digest tests disagree. | Establish and record a fresh full baseline before feature changes; fix or quarantine only evidenced pre-existing failures; run targeted and full suites in pinned WSL env. | Separate inherited failures from new work; do not claim a green package until full verification passes. |
| T-10 | Open-SWE processed artifacts cannot support enrichment. | certain | high | Only hashes/lengths remain; no task/test oracle or repository join. | Stream governed raw Parquet into a new versioned derived build; use only for offline prevalence/recognizer/evaluator validity. | Sample a smaller raw subset or drop semantic offline analyses; no live causal claim. |

## 3. Research and interpretation risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Severity | Early trigger | Mitigation | Honest result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R-01 | H1 does not meet the locked five-point observed repeat-harm rule against the selected scalar controller. | high | high | Any repeat-harm `Delta` point estimate is <0.05, any required notice/behaviour simultaneous one-sided lower bound is ≤0, a notice validity gate fails, or a utility co-gate fails. | Power from pooled/blinded pilot nuisance quantities; precise high-base-rate motifs; preserve all comparators, notice criteria, margins, and intersection–union decision. | Report that richer persistent state did not demonstrate the locked practical/statistical advantage. A repeat-harm point estimate ≥0.05 with lower bound >0 does not imply the true effect is ≥0.05. |
| R-02 | H1 holds but H2 is null: state actuation, persistence, or wiring is unsupported. | medium | critical | Influence-off is equivalent to intact; persistence reset retains the advantage; clamps do not move first intent/outcome; null-equivalence gate fails. | Randomized Influence-off/dose/update-off/persistence-off/permutation; powered stochastic sham/no-op/restore equivalence checks. | Publish the negative causal conclusion; do not relabel correlation as mechanism or credit persistence without the reset result. |
| R-03 | Repeat reduction is bought with over-caution. | medium | critical | Task success or counterfactual strategy use crosses NI margin. | H5 co-gates and resource/utility frontier; explicit tasks where old strategy is now correct. | State reduces recurrence but harms useful discrimination; H1 fails. |
| R-04 | Effect is model/size specific. | medium | high | 7B and 1.5B differ in sign or one cannot follow structured candidates. | Primary 7B stratum; local same-family 1.5B robustness; never pool; model×effect interaction reported. | Scope to the checkpoint that was actually tested. |
| R-05 | Suite-A effect does not transfer to the scoped real-repository population. | high | high | B-live and B-offline signals disagree with controlled tasks or lineage-cluster power is inadequate. | Restrict B-live to preregistered mechanically scorable known motifs and frozen repository/prototype population; report lineage power; surface/repo transforms and offline prevalence/observability audit. | Controlled-setting result only; real-repository transfer is negative/inconclusive and natural-SWE generalization remains unresolved. |
| R-06 | “Notice” endpoint repeats E-0 or measures an outcome-dependent target. | high | high | The carrier-matched `NoticeReadout` has poor discrimination/calibration/coverage, loses to any of the five H1 comparators, violates equal measurement compute, or its label changes after the action. | Freeze the generator-held recurrence fact before action; balance target/decoy/counterfactual opportunities; use one inert schema and identical actor checkpoint, current observables, prompt/decoding/timing/call count, and measurement-token cap across all seven arms while exposing only each arm's legitimate carrier; meter measurement separately; keep `NoticeProbability` separate from `L_m`, update signal, and evaluator. | Behaviour may change without validated noticing; do not claim the agent noticed recurrence. |

## 5. Current top-ten watchlist

1. **M-02** — treatment/outcome label leakage.
2. **M-01** — treatment-dependent exposure/denominator regression.
3. **M-03** — invalid frozen descendants.
4. **M-04** — missing or unbeatable four-family scalar persistent control.
5. **T-01** — no equal live structured-action driver yet.
6. **T-02** — exact motif observability/measurement error.
7. **M-06** — E-0 activity/context confound.
8. **M-05** — lineage/assignment/multiplicity misspecification.
9. **T-04** — executable sandbox acquisition.
10. **P-01** — deadline versus zero accepted empirical tasks at the audited base.

The first official run is no-go while any of M-01–M-05, M-12–M-14, T-01–T-03,
T-08, or O-01 lacks its listed gate. Negative expected results R-01–R-06 never block
execution once measurement and causal validity are sound.
