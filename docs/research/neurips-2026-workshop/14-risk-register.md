# 14 — Risk Register

Status: planning artifact. Consistent with `02-research-thesis.md` §8 (Locked
Design Constants) and `01-current-state-audit.md`. This register enumerates the
risks that could sink the first paper (IAB @ NeurIPS 2026, deadline 2026-08-29),
sourced from the three code/doc audits. Nothing here authorizes implementation.

Every risk carries: `id | category | description | likelihood | impact |
detection signal | mitigation | contingency`. Scales are `low / med / high`.
Rows are grouped by the four categories and, within each category, ordered by
impact (high first). A cross-category impact ranking closes the document (§6).

Categories:

- **Technical (T)** — the instrument, harness, detector, or environment fails to
  do the mechanical thing the claim needs.
- **Methodological (M)** — the experiment runs but a confound, weak baseline, or
  circular construction lets a reviewer reject the causal/behavioural claim.
- **Research (R)** — the underlying hypothesis is false, un-earnable on real
  data, or out of governance scope.
- **Publication (P)** — the paper cannot be produced, defended, or accepted on
  time and to venue standard.

## 2. Methodological risks

| id   | category       | description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | likelihood | impact | detection signal                                                                                                                                                                | mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | contingency                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M-01 | methodological | **E-0 confound (the headline threat).** In-repo E-0 pre-registered 3 hypotheses and failed all three: replayed psyche signals scored AUROC 0.339/0.349 at predicting failure vs a retry-count baseline of 0.705, anti-correlated via a length/activity confound (few-step early terminations vs long busy successful runs). Risk: measured RUF differences between arms are token/context/activity confounds, not the internal state. | high       | high   | Arms differ in token/action counts; RUF gap tracks trajectory length or context size rather than the intervened variable; retry-count controller matches or beats Pneuma-state. | Include the retry-count heuristic controller as a mandatory strong baseline (falsification guard); tightly match token and action budgets across all arms; covariate-adjust RUF for length/activity; pre-register the DV as _behavioural repeat-reduction under causal gating_, not passive prediction.    | If a confound survives adjustment, publish as a negative/inconclusive result with the confound characterized (E-0 is the house-style precedent: recorded negatives are first-class).        |
| M-02 | methodological | **Circular / co-designed subject.** The only L4 result in existence is L4 of a hand-coded `ReferencePsyche` co-designed with its own fixtures and hypotheses — "circular by construction." A subject co-authored with its evaluator proves the harness, not a mind.                                                                                                                                                                   | high       | high   | The only passing subject is the reference psyche; the hollow/narrator baseline is absent from a run; reviewers flag "you built the answer key."                                 | Run on a real (non-co-designed) stochastic agent; keep `HollowPsyche` as a standing ablate-everything control; add an LLM narrator baseline (psyche-shaped frames without the mechanism) — if the suite can't separate real from narrator, freeze the claim.                                               | If a real subject cannot be stood up, restrict all claims to "methodology validated; mechanism demonstrated on a reference mind" and make the circularity caveat explicit and load-bearing. |
| M-03 | methodological | **Weak-baseline accusation.** Reviewers reject H1 as "just Reflexion / just retrieval" if the memory/reflection baselines are strawmen. A weak retrieval or a toy reflection arm invalidates the contrast.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | high       | high   | Retrieval baseline uses trivial top-k with no reranking; reflection arm is a stub; baseline Pass@1 far below published Reflexion/SWE-Exp numbers.                               | Implement a strong retrieval baseline on `foundation/memory.py` (SQLite/FTS5, proper top-k) and a real Reflexion/ExpeL-style reflection arm under identical budget, tools, retry cap, and task order; report baseline task-success against published anchors.                                              | If a baseline underperforms its literature anchor, disclose the gap and temper the novelty claim to the causal-clamp differentiator (which no baseline has).                                |
| M-04 | methodological | **Over-caution / false-avoidance.** Pneuma-state could buy RUF reduction merely by becoming inert (refusing the previously-punished strategy even when it is now correct) — reduction bought by inertia, not by useful learning (H5 threat).                                                                                                                                                                                          | high       | med    | Task-success or first-attempt-success drops relative to the best memory/reflection baseline; false-avoidance rate exceeds reflection on counterfactual tasks.                   | Add counterfactual tasks where a previously-punished strategy is now correct; measure a false-avoidance metric; require Pneuma-state success not below the best baseline and false-avoidance not worse than reflection.                                                                                    | If false-avoidance is worse, report H5-false honestly (reduction is bought by inertia) — a publishable negative bound on the mechanism.                                                     |
| M-05 | methodological | **Single-model artifact.** H1 could be an artifact of one base model / one size; a reviewer asks "does it replicate at another scale?"                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | med        | high   | Effect present at the pinned checkpoint but untested elsewhere; no robustness section.                                                                                          | Reproduce H1 on ≥2 model sizes (DL-01 mandates model-agnostic design); pin exact checkpoints in the run config.                                                                                                                                                                                            | If a second size is infeasible under compute, scope the claim to the tested model and flag single-model as an explicit limitation, not a silent gap.                                        |
| M-06 | methodological | **Self-report / voice contamination.** If self-report quality (H3) leaks into the behavioural score, a persuasive "diary entry" inflates the result — the exact decorative-narration failure the program is built to avoid.                                                                                                                                                                                                           | low        | high   | Any code path from voice/self-report into the RUF scorer; behavioural score changes when only prose changes.                                                                    | Score behaviour with the audited prose-blind evaluator (verified one-way scorer↔voice firewall); test self-report faithfulness separately; bind reports to same-tick state hashes; keep the four report conditions (template / unconstrained-LLM / grounded / grounded+verified) off the behavioural path. | If any leak is found, invalidate affected runs and re-score prose-blind; the firewall is already audited-clean, so this is a regression guard.                                              |
| M-07 | methodological | **Motif-label subjectivity.** RUF depends on "same underlying failure" judgments; if motif labels are one annotator's opinion, the primary metric is unfalsifiable.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | med        | med    | Inter-annotator agreement on motif labels is low; RUF swings with the labeller.                                                                                                 | Use an N-judge ensemble for motif labels (different providers, digests-only, swap-tested); report agreement; treat LLM annotation as data, not gate.                                                                                                                                                       | If agreement is low on some motifs, restrict RUF to the high-agreement motif subset and report the rest descriptively.                                                                      |

## 3. Research risks

| id   | category | description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | likelihood | impact | detection signal                                                                                                                                                         | mitigation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | contingency                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| R-01 | research | **"Persistent" is aspirational.** `reset()` clears scars; cross-run persistence has only ever been shown tautologically (same fixture motif every tick, fixed +0.1). True longitudinal persistence — the paper's independent variable — has never been demonstrated in this repo or 9to5 (`psyche.db` never existed on disk). The longitudinal claim may not hold on real data. | high       | high   | On real multi-task sequences, scar state does not carry across tasks, or carries but does not shape later behaviour; dose-response on accumulated scar strength is flat. | Build genuine cross-run persistence (durable scar store surviving task boundaries) with decay + cap; pre-register the dose-response prediction (effect scales monotonically with accumulated scar strength); measure persistence on a real task sequence, not a fixed fixture.    | If persistence doesn't hold on real data, that is itself a first-class negative result (state does not durably carry) — publish it; the E-0 ethic supports recorded negatives. |
| R-02 | research | **H2 causal null: state is epiphenomenal.** Even if H1 holds, ablating/clamping the failure-memory variable may leave RUF unchanged (`RUF(pneuma_ablated) ≈ RUF(pneuma)`) — the state correlates with but does not cause the reduction.                                                                                                                                         | med        | high   | Treated-minus-null contrast is non-significant; ablation does not move RUF; null arm fails to reproduce control.                                                         | Run the control/treated/null paired design with the clamp on the failure-memory variable; require the null to reproduce control and the ablation to remove the reduction; report dose-response.                                                                                   | Publish as a **negative causal result** ("internal state correlates with but does not cause the reduction") — explicitly named as publishable in `02` §6.                      |
| R-03 | research | **Synthetic-task overfitting.** A win only on injected-motif synthetic repos has insufficient real-world relevance; reviewers discount it as toy.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | med        | high   | Effect strong on synthetic suite but absent/weak on real-repo suite (Open-SWE-Traces, SWE-Gym OpenHands-Sampled).                                                        | Run the dual suite: synthetic controlled-motif (exact ground truth) AND real-repo (natural recurring-failure distribution); require the effect to survive on real repos as the load-bearing claim; synthetic gives clean causal control, real gives external validity.            | If the effect is synthetic-only, report it as a controlled-setting demonstration with an explicit external-validity limitation, not a general result.                          |
| R-04 | research | **Generalization / memorization (H4).** The reduction could be identifier memorization that vanishes under surface variation or on held-out repos/motifs. Motif-axis splitting does NOT exist today (repo axis only).                                                                                                                                                           | med        | high   | Effect present in-distribution, gone under surface-varied instances or held-out repos/motifs.                                                                            | Build motif-axis splitting (a foundational task) in addition to repo-grouped splits; test on surface-varied instances and held-out motifs; quarantine the 7 repos overlapping between Open-SWE-Traces and SWE-Gym per the leakage registry.                                       | If generalization fails, report H4-false (memorization not generalization) as a bounded, honest finding.                                                                       |
| R-05 | research | **Benchmark contamination.** SWE-bench-family task identifiers may sit in the base model's pretraining, so apparent competence is recall, not reasoning — and could differentially advantage some arms.                                                                                                                                                                         | med        | med    | Suspiciously high resolve rate on canonical SWE-bench instances; performance drops on a post-cutoff pair.                                                                | Mandatory contamination disclaimer on all SWE-bench-family results; include a post-cutoff pair (e.g. swe-mera/swe-evo); minhash near-dup scan; repo-grouped deterministic splits.                                                                                                 | If contamination is evident, lean the headline on synthetic (contamination-free by construction) and post-cutoff real tasks; disclose the SWE-bench caveat prominently.        |
| R-06 | research | **Compute / data governance.** Everything is `training_weight: 0.0` / `not_authorized`; the paper does no weight training of the subject. If a downstream step silently requires authorized training or unavailable compute, the plan stalls.                                                                                                                                   | med        | med    | Any pipeline step demands `authorized` state or a training run; `authorization_review` gates fail closed.                                                                | Keep the entire design inference-only (frozen base model, no subject weight training); confirm every lane stays `not_authorized`/`training_weight 0.0`; use `foundation/memory.py` retrieval which needs no GPU/auth; the local Qwen subject is explicitly off the critical path. | If any authorized step turns out necessary, cut it from the paper scope rather than seek authorization under deadline.                                                         |

## 5. Cross-cutting mitigations (apply to many rows)

- **Retry-count controller as a standing baseline** (M-01) doubles as a
  falsification guard for the whole program (`02` §6).
- **Prose-blind firewall + hollow/narrator controls** (M-02, M-06) are the
  reusable anti-gaming spine — verified one-way today.
- **Pre-registration + no-sign-flip discipline** (from E-0) applies to every
  hypothesis; recorded negatives are first-class (R-01, R-02, R-04, M-04).
- **Commit+config+seed binding** (P-03) is the provenance substrate every claim
  inherits.
