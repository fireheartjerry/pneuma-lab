## Open decisions deferred to leakage-safe development (flagged, not yet made)

- **Exact model artifacts and serving build:** Qwen2.5-Coder-7B is the primary
  family and 1.5B the robustness family, but quantization hashes, inference
  backend, and sampler/seed compatibility are frozen only after the local seed
  audit and before confirmatory split access.
- **Number of independent prototype lineages:** selected by simulation using the
  disjoint pilot's blinded variance/correlation estimates. Under DL-33, the
  declared design alternative is `Delta_power = 0.10` for every H1 superiority
  component, and the selected count must yield at least 80% probability that the
  complete H1 conjunction passes under the frozen joint DGP. This is not power
  at the 0.05 observed SESOI. Surface variants, challenges, and seeds never
  increase the independent-unit count.
- **Scalar hazard-controller form:** last-failure bit, count, EWMA, and Beta-
  posterior candidates are compared on development data under exactly the same
  current-only recognizer, learning signal, action head, persistence scope, and
  at least the same trial budget as Pneuma, then frozen with the full ledger.
- **B-live repository/container roster:** frozen after acquisition, licensing,
  deterministic-verifier, resource, and isolation smoke tests. It cannot be
  chosen using arm outcomes.
- **Exploratory secondary family:** the exact BH-FDR metric list is frozen in the
  preregistration. Confirmatory family construction is locked by DL-25 and
  DL-29; H5 is an embedded H1 co-gate rather than a duplicate global test.
