This records every major design decision, its alternatives, and its rationale.
Rows DL-01–DL-15 are the original Protocol-v1 history; DL-16–DL-41 are the
Protocol-v2 decisions and explicitly supersede earlier rows where noted.
`02-research-thesis.md` remains the live scientific contract. When several
plausible options exist, we analyze the trade-offs, choose
the most ambitious technically coherent option that preserves validity and
testability, and record the rejected alternatives here.

## Open decisions deferred to leakage-safe development (flagged, not yet made)

- **Exact model artifacts and serving build:** Qwen2.5-Coder-7B is the primary
  family and 1.5B the robustness family, but quantization hashes, inference
  backend, and sampler/seed compatibility are frozen only after the local seed
  audit and before confirmatory split access.
- **Number of independent prototype lineages:** selected by simulation using the
  disjoint pilot's blinded variance/correlation estimates. Under DL-33, the
  declared design alternative is `Delta_power = 0.10` for every H1 superiority
  component, and the selected count must yield at least 80% probability that the
  complete H1 conjunction passes under the frozen joint DGP. This is not power
  at the 0.05 observed SESOI. Surface variants, challenges, and seeds never
  increase the independent-unit count.
- **Scalar hazard-controller form:** last-failure bit, count, EWMA, and Beta-
  posterior candidates are compared on development data under exactly the same
  current-only recognizer, learning signal, action head, persistence scope, and
  at least the same trial budget as Pneuma, then frozen with the full ledger.
- **B-live repository/container roster:** frozen after acquisition, licensing,
  deterministic-verifier, resource, and isolation smoke tests. It cannot be
  chosen using arm outcomes.
- **Exploratory secondary family:** the exact BH-FDR metric list is frozen in the
  preregistration. Confirmatory family construction is locked by DL-25 and
  DL-29; H5 is an embedded H1 co-gate rather than a duplicate global test.

## Deviations logged by downstream documents

Protocol v2 was adopted after an independent scientific/repository red-team on
2026-07-22. These changes are mandatory before confirmatory execution.

| id    | supersedes | chosen | rationale |
| ----- | ---------- | ------ | --------- |
| DL-16 | DL-06, DL-08 | Common pre-treatment verified failure exposure plus a fixed, pre-scheduled post-exposure opportunity denominator; natural end-to-end recurrence is secondary | Removes post-treatment eligibility/denominator selection. An arm that avoids every failure remains defined and receives the best possible recurrence value rather than being dropped. |
| DL-17 | DL-07, DL-10 | Separate actor-side prospective recognizer, actor-side online update signal, and arm-blinded offline evaluator with no shared learned parameters or test-set threshold tuning | The retrospective detector cannot prospectively prevent a failure, and using one label path for treatment and outcome creates circular evidence. |
| DL-18 | DL-09 | Clone only the common prefix and initial sandbox; freeze checkpoint/config/exogenous seeds, then run every post-intervention branch live | Later prompts, files, tool outputs, and state are descendants of behavior. | DL-19 | DL-03 | Seven core conditions: Base, Retrieval, Reflection, Retry-count, matched persistent Motif-count/EMA, Pneuma-state, and all-state Influence-off; optional State×Retrieval factorial | A cross-task motif-count/EMA controller is the strongest simple explanation for `s_m` and is mandatory. | DL-20 | DL-04, DL-05 | Retrieval-free four-variable state (`s_m`, calibrated `c`, boundary-time caution `t`, recognizer reliability `r_m`) drives bounded reranking of model-proposed structured candidate actions | Removes the treatment contradiction around memory trust, makes neutral state exactly zero, prevents token/action-count decay confounds, and preserves “pressure, not commands.” |
