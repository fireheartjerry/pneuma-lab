**Standing sentences** (quote them, do not paraphrase them weaker):

- The only Level-4 result in existence is Level 4 of a hand-coded reference
  implementation inside its own harness — it validates the methodology, not any mind.
- No wire connects the three repos at runtime; the 9to5→PneumaTrace exporter is the
  single highest-leverage unbuilt component.
